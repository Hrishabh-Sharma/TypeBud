Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Effective immediately

100% Client-Side Privacy Guarantee: TypeBud is engineered to operate strictly within your local browser. We do not track, collect, transmit, or monetize your data.

1. Custom PDF & Document Processing

When you upload a PDF or text template to practice typing, all text extraction and processing takes place exclusively inside your browser's local JavaScript memory. No document content, file bytes, or text are ever transmitted to any external server or third-party service.

2. Local Storage Usage

TypeBud uses your browser's standard localStorage solely to save your personal typing records, beginner lesson progress, avatar image, and your sound preference. This data remains on your machine and can be cleared at any time via the Personal Records interface.

3. No Cookies, No Telemetry, No Third-Party Trackers

TypeBud contains zero analytics trackers (e.g., Google Analytics), zero tracking cookies, zero external advertising scripts, and zero third-party font or asset CDNs. It is fully functional offline.
